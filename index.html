    Where you live changes your footprint

    Dorm life, campus-sized

    • Use a switched power strip for your desk and turn it off when you leave. Chargers and monitors keep drawing power on standby.
    • Share one mini-fridge with your roommate. A fridge runs 24/7, roughly 30 kWh a month, so two is a waste.
    • Wash full loads in cold water and skip the dryer when you can. Most laundry energy goes to heat.
    • Unplug the fridge and electronics before breaks, and ask your RA about move-out checklists.
    • Set laptops and monitors to sleep after 5 minutes, and keep windows shut while the heat or AC runs. Report drafty ones.
    • Start a floor challenge. Many campuses run dorm-vs-dorm energy contests, so ask your RA or sustainability office.
